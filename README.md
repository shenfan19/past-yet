# Temporal Agency

Working title. You run a small staffing agency that sends people into the past. History cannot be changed, so every trip creates a branch timeline. The staff come home with stories, gifts and time energy, and you spend the energy on getting back to your own family.

It is a travel-frog style game: send people out, wait, read what happened. Each trip is a small life simulation driven by traits and the conditions of an era, run on models from [Life Matters](https://github.com/shenfan19). Each era also comes with a short encyclopedia entry on what happened and what was lost.
